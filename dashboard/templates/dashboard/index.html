Read the network as evidence

CAPMF stringency is a relative index. The 0–10 scale is not a physical quantity or a measure of emissions avoided. Higher-level scores are published aggregates; this dashboard never reconstructs them from incomplete child observations.

Taxonomy membership · grey guides
Explicit code mappings connect policies to text-only parent group anchors. Parent policy nodes are not drawn, so only one hierarchy level appears at a time. These guides are definitional.
Statistical association · blue lines
Spearman correlation uses paired, non-missing annual scores within the window. Ties receive average ranks; constant series and fewer than three paired years produce no correlation. Only positive correlations at or above the threshold are drawn. Short windows and shared trends can produce unstable associations.
Possible succession · amber arrows
A positive-to-zero transition is paired with another instrument’s first positive observation within two years, in the same sector or family. Both events must be inside the window; the predecessor must have become active first. This is a hypothesis for expert review, not proof of replacement.
Documented lineage · purple links
Optional, cited edges preserve all supplied parents. Directed cycles are rejected. Source and target event years can be supplied to validate temporal order; without them chronology remains unverified. Red conflict links are symmetric and excluded from the DAG check, as are statistical and taxonomy connections.

Window and change conventions

Window width is always exactly W years before clipping to data bounds. For an even centred window, the extra year precedes the anchor (W = 4: Y − 2 to Y + 1). The symbol next to each node shows entry (+), exit (−), strengthening (↑), weakening (↓), sustained activity (=), or incomplete coverage (?). Net changes of at least 0.5 score points receive an arrow; this is a descriptive threshold, not equal policy effort. Node area shows the score at the anchor year. Dormant and unknown nodes remain available as context.

Missingness and estimates

Zero is retained. Missing observations are never filled. M (not applicable) and K (merged category) are treated as unavailable for analysis. Estimated observations have dashed borders; the exclusion switch makes E values unavailable for every calculation. An OECD aggregate may already incorporate imputation even when some component observations are absent.

Forward commitments

Variables ending in DATE contain scores, not calendar years. Forward links require a separate, cited target file containing policy_id, commitment_year, target_year and source_citation. The optional 2050 example refers to Ireland’s 2021 Climate Act amendment, not an inferred date from CAPMF. It is shown only where the commitment is known by the anchor year and the instrument is active in the window.

Colour accessibility

The six sector hues use the Okabe–Ito palette. Sector headings, spatial grouping and text labels preserve the category independently of colour. The bundled QA note records full-severity protanopia and deuteranopia simulations; closely spaced colours must be read with their labels.
